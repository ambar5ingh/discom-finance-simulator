# Data notice

The MIT licence covers the **code** only.

The files in `data/` hold individual figures compiled by the author from the publications listed in
`data/sources.csv`. Every row carries a `source_id`. No tables, text or figures from those
publications are reproduced. Anyone reusing these numbers should cite the original source named
in each row.

- `G1`: the single government source used for official statistics (Lok Sabha reply, 18 Dec 2025).
- `C1`: CSEP, *Breaking Down the Gap in DisCom Finances* (2023). Copyright © CSEP. Cited, not redistributed.
- `DESIGN`: modelling assumptions made for this simulator. These are not sourced facts.

or the author's employer.
